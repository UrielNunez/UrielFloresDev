About Me

A little about me and the tools behind my work.

Code, worlds & everything in between.

I'm Uriel, a Software Engineer who moves between web development, Unity and 3D. I build internal business tools, games and AR/VR experiences—from understanding what people need to designing the interface, writing the code and releasing a working product.

At CEPSA I turn operational processes into web systems. At GatoLobo I worked on gameplay, missions and UI. My independent projects take me through modeling, rigging and interactive prototypes. Teaching development at UNITEC made me more deliberate about explaining technical decisions and making complex ideas feel approachable.

The thread through all of it is curiosity: I like to learn how something works, then make it useful and enjoyable for someone else.
